How it works

Three steps. Then you close the app.

  1. Write what matters

    Cap the list at five. If it doesn’t fit, it waits until tomorrow.

  2. Pick one focus

    Harbor highlights a single job so the rest of the day has an anchor.

  3. Check off and stop

    When the list is done, Harbor closes. No streak guilt. No extra tabs.
